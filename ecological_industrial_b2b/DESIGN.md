## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a corporate, authoritative, yet profoundly ecological aesthetic engineered for industrial waste management, environmental engineering, and biomedical waste (DASRI) treatment in West Africa and emerging industrial hubs. 

The visual direction follows a **Corporate / Modern Ecological** approach:
- **Tone & Mood:** Institutional rigor, uncompromising compliance, certified hygiene, and progressive environmental stewardship. It must inspire confidence among enterprise executives, plant directors, health facilities, and public authorities.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous, airy layouts backed by ultra-soft botanical and cool slate tints; structured technical typography with tight tracking in headers; precision-crafted borders instead of heavy shadows; and disciplined, crisp badge and icon treatments reflecting industrial certification standards.
- **Imagery & Iconography:** Authentic operational photography (PPE equipment, compliant thermal incinerators, logistics fleets, laboratory tracking) paired with clean 2px-stroke geometric vector iconography and duotone badges.

## Colors

The color palette pairs the grounding reliability of certified environmental greens with the procedural authority of industrial maritime and deep slate blues.

### Hierarchy & Functional Assignment
- **Primary Green (`#15803D` / Deep Forest `#166534`):** Anchors core actions, key brand headings, active navigation indicators, and verified compliance markers.
- **Secondary Blue (`#0369A1` / Sky-Industrial `#0284C7`):** Represents industrial rigor, technical methodology, chemical safety, water protection, and secondary interactive flows (diagnostics, certifications).
- **Tertiary Accent (`#22C55E`):** High-visibility ecological accent reserved for micro-interactions, success states, and operational badges.
- **Dark Neutral (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal/slate for high-legibility typographic hierarchies and institutional footer foundations. Avoid absolute pure black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Foundations:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) on elevated cards, alternating with mint-white tint (`#F0FDF4`) and neutral slate wash (`#F8FAFC`) to partition long multi-tier dashboards and institutional pages without visual fatigue.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle, low-contrast borders (`#E2E8F0` and `#DCFCE7`) maintain visual discipline without hard boxiness.

## Typography

The typography uses **Manrope** across all roles to achieve a balance between engineering precision and modern geometric clarity.

- **Headings:** Bold to extra-bold with slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates unified, authoritative headlines that command respect in tenders, institutional documentation, and executive overviews.
- **Body Text:** Neutral, highly readable regular weight (`400`) calibrated with open line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to ensure effortless reading of technical protocols, waste category definitions, and compliance procedures.
- **Section Eyebrows & Overlines:** Rendered in `label-caps` (`11px` to `12px`, uppercase, `0.1em` letter spacing, weight `800`) in primary green or deep blue to introduce sections with institutional structure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for enterprise clarity:
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, max container width `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` side margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins.

### Section Rhythm & Stacking
Section padding follows generous alternating patterns: major content modules feature `5rem` to `6rem` vertical padding on desktop, scaling down to `3rem` on mobile. White cards on mint (`#F0FDF4`) or soft slate (`#F8FAFC`) backgrounds provide clean, segmented breathing room for services and methodology stages.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical or heavy multi-directional shadows in favor of certified institutional precision:
- **Elevation Level 0 (Base Surface):** Flat cards with a 1px solid stroke in `#E2E8F0` or `#DCFCE7` with zero blur.
- **Elevation Level 1 (Card Rest):** Used on service cards and diagnostic interactive modules. A hairline border (`1px solid #F1F5F9`) combined with an ultra-soft tinted ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.03)`.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Hover / Active Focus):** On interaction, cards smoothly elevate with `transform: translateY(-3px)` and an ambient shadow of `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Flyouts:** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)` with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a `#0F172A` overlay at 45% opacity.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced radius hierarchy (`roundedness: 2` base of `0.5rem` / `8px`) that balances industrial discipline with approachable ecological warmth:
- **Buttons, Badges & Pills:** Full rounded pills (`9999px`) for CTA buttons, status labels, and icon containers to provide contrast against rectangular cards.
- **Cards, Modals & Diagnostic Panels:** Formed with `0.75rem` (`12px`) or `1rem` (`16px`) corners (`rounded-lg`), delivering a clean, modern enclosure.
- **Inner Controls (Form inputs, select boxes):** Styled with `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners for crisp alignment with industrial standards.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Devis / Diagnostic):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid background in `#15803D`, high-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`), `14px` weight `700`, padded with `12px 24px`. Subtle transition to `#166534` on hover with forward arrow micro-animation (`translateX(3px)`).
- **Secondary Action (En savoir plus / Services):** Pill-shaped with a white surface, `1.5px` border in `#15803D`, and text in `#15803D`. On hover, fills with `#F0FDF4`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Borderless, deep blue (`#0369A1`) or charcoal (`#0F172A`), underlined only on hover.

### 2. Service & Industrial Cards
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), rounded `12px` to `16px`, bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top section accommodates an operational photo with a subtle border or an icon enclosed in an organic circular badge (`#F0FDF4` fill with `#15803D` icon).
- Bottom contains a bold title, concise description, and an icon button (pill or circle) at bottom-left or bottom-right to guide the user into service specifics.

### 3. Step Process / Methodology Stepper
- Horizontal timeline connected by muted dashed or continuous lines (`#CBD5E1`).
- Step nodes: Numbered circular badges (`36px` diameter) in deep green (`#15803D`) with bold white numbering, paired with top indicator badges that highlight icons for diagnosis, transit, sorting, treatment, and traceability.

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** `44px` height, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `8px` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` background. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies an active border in `#15803D` with a subtle focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` square/circle, `#15803D` active fill with crisp white checkmark.

### 5. Compliance & Trust Badges
- Pill-shaped chips (`padding: 6px 14px`, `12px` label font) using `#F0FDF4` fill, `#15803D` label, and `1px solid #DCFCE7` border.
- Dedicated partner and client certification carousel modules utilizing grayscale logos with a transition to full color on cursor interaction.
